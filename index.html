About

Saffron & Stone is a luxury residential interior design studio creating timeless, thoughtfully curated homes that feel both elevated and deeply personal. Rooted in a refined, earthy aesthetic, we blend natural materials, rich textures, and warm, nuanced palettes to craft spaces that are as functional as they are beautiful.

Bhavana Bhimavarapu

Founder / Design Principal

Creating a home is about so much more than selecting beautiful finishes or furnishings—it's about designing a space that reflects your story and supports the way you live. That belief has guided my career from the very beginning and ultimately inspired me to create Saffron & Stone.

For more than a decade, I've had the privilege of working at one of the region's leading residential design firms. During those years, I had the privilege of working on custom new builds, large-scale renovations, and beautifully layered interiors alongside talented architects, builders, craftsmen, and industry professionals. Those experiences shaped not only my design perspective but also my belief that exceptional homes are created through collaboration, attention to detail, and a process that feels effortless for the client.

The name Saffron & Stone is deeply personal to me. Saffron is a tribute to my Indian heritage, symbolizing warmth, richness, and tradition. Stone represents strength, timelessness, and enduring craftsmanship. Together, they embody the foundation of my work—creating homes that are refined yet approachable, elevated yet comfortable, and designed to stand the test of time.

When I'm not designing, you'll most likely find me exploring new destinations with my family, drawing inspiration from architecture, culture, and local craftsmanship around the world—or happily chasing after my three energetic kids, who remind me every day that the best homes are designed to be lived in, celebrated, and filled with memories.

Services

Saffron & Stone is a full-service interior design studio offering a comprehensive, detail-driven approach to creating refined and deeply personal homes. We work across new construction, large-scale renovations, and full-home furnishings—managing every phase from initial concept through final installation. Our process is designed to be seamless and highly tailored, allowing each project to unfold with clarity, intention, and ease.

Full-Service Interior Design

Our full-service offering is designed for clients seeking a complete, start-to-finish experience. We oversee every detail of the design process, including:

  • •Space planning and layout development
  • •Material and finish selections
  • •Custom furniture and millwork design
  • •Lighting design and specifications
  • •Procurement, order management, and vendor coordination
  • •Styling and final installation

This service ensures a cohesive, elevated result—where every element is thoughtfully considered and executed.

New Construction & Renovations

We collaborate closely with architects, builders, and contractors to guide projects from the ground up or through transformative remodels. Our role includes:

  • •Reviewing architectural plans and providing design direction
  • •Selecting interior architectural details and finishes
  • •Designing kitchens, bathrooms, and built-ins
  • •Ensuring continuity between structure and interior design
  • •Supporting on-site decisions throughout construction

Our goal is to bring both vision and precision—ensuring the finished home feels intentional, balanced, and timeless.

Furnishings & Styling

For clients looking to elevate an existing space, we offer a full furnishings and styling service. This includes:

  • •Furniture layouts and curated selections
  • •Custom upholstery and casegoods
  • •Rugs, lighting, and window treatments
  • •Art, accessories, and finishing details
  • •White-glove delivery and installation

We approach furnishings with the same level of care as full-scale projects—creating spaces that feel layered, complete, and effortlessly refined.
