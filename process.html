Process

How We Work

Our six-step process is designed to be seamless and highly tailored—guiding you from initial vision to a finished home with clarity, intention, and ease.

01

Initial Consultation

Every project begins with understanding your vision, lifestyle, and goals. We take the time to learn how you live so we can create a home that feels uniquely yours.

02

Design Development

We transform ideas into a cohesive design through thoughtful planning, curated selections, and detailed presentations that bring your vision to life.

03

Documentation & Coordination

From design drawings to communication with architects, builders, and trades, we ensure every detail is carefully documented and seamlessly coordinated.

04

Procurement

We manage every aspect of purchasing, order tracking, vendor communication, and logistics, providing a streamlined experience from selection to delivery.

05

Installation & Styling

As each piece comes together, we oversee installation and thoughtfully style every space to create a home that feels complete, layered, and effortlessly refined.

06

Welcome Home

The final reveal is the culmination of a carefully guided process, resulting in a home that reflects your story and is designed to be enjoyed for years to come.
